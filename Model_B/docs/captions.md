# Figure captions and file map

All current PNG figures are 600 dpi. Current A/B/C comparison figures use the same 521 junctions; the optimization figures retain B's 530-junction search population. Blue denotes a reduction in the target response; red/orange denotes an increase. These are model outputs, not measured surface inundation.

- Figure 5 — design/fig/ABC_color.png. Original A, cost-constrained B and C201, with common diameter classes, invert-elevation classes and outlet symbols. Colored conduit overlays denote enlargement relative to A. No new conduits were added. Invert elevation is not burial depth.
- Figure 6 — current/fig/F4_all.png. Changes in maximum ponded depth and accumulated flooding duration for B versus A, C201 versus A, and C201 versus B. Values are reference minus target; positive blue values indicate reductions. Full observed symmetric scales are shared by metric; all nine potential outlet nodes are excluded.
- Figure S1 — F1_search.png. Recorded objective minima, selected compromise, archive hypervolume and numerical screening through 50 NSGA-II generations. Objective-wise minima may describe different designs; the selected objective uses all 530 A/B junctions.
- Figure S2 — F2_front.png. Feasible evaluated designs, cumulative archive evolution and four final nondominated alternatives, with the selected B identified. Color denotes pipe-replacement component cost.
- Figure S3 — F9_all.png. Node-level response classes using 0.01 ft and one-minute tolerances. Lower and higher refer to the target model; tolerances are descriptive, not significance tests.
- Figure S4 — F3_nodes.png. Paired node maxima/durations and empirical cumulative distributions for A, B and C201. All 521 common junctions are included, with zeros for unflooded junctions.
- Figure S5 — F6_event.png. Shared rainfall, mean instantaneous modeled ponding, total outfall discharge and number of ponded common junctions. C201 discharge sums its six outfalls; curves are sampled at 60 s. The three-hour window ends with substantial ponding remaining.
- Figure S6 — Model E/current/fig/E_cost.png. Component cost, hydraulic response, nonconvergence diagnostic and the former 200 m3 design boundary. All 126 layouts are priced; 94 have primary simulations, of which 93 are within the cost allowance.
- Figure S7 — E_near.png. The 13 configurations including C201 within 95-100% of the component allowance, with added geometric pipe volume and numerical diagnostics. C201 is case e044; other 12 configurations are alternatives.
- Figure S8 — E_hier.png. Relative Pareto-target deviation versus hydraulic means for 93 cost-admissible layouts. Color denotes cost. Relationships are descriptive and do not isolate the effect of hierarchy.
- Figure S9 — E_nodes.png. Illustrative node differences for e011 minus C201. Here blue negative values indicate reduction and red positive values increase. Both primary runs exceed the nonconvergence screen. The illustration is not a certified ranking.
- Additional F5_pipes.png — B pipe enlargement and replacement-cost allocation. M_AB_d/t, M_AC_d/t and M_BC_d/t are individual continuous-map panels; K_* are corresponding classified panels.
